What this cannot do

A Chrome extension is sandboxed to the browser. It cannot enumerate the software installed on your operating system, read files, or see traffic from other applications, so the structural pass covers the browser's own attack surface: the site you are on and the extensions you have installed. That is also where most consumer-facing compromise actually happens. Reaching the OS would require a companion native application and a separate, explicit install.

Scores are screening signals produced by pattern analysis and list matching. They are not verdicts about any person or company, and nothing here is legal or financial advice.
